# Implied EOs: the full list

All {= n(len(SETS)) =} sets of scrambles with two or more EOs of 5 moves or less. Every EO in a row is on a scramble exactly when the others in the row are. The seven kinds are those of [the write-up](./), which has the theorems behind them.

How to read a row. An EO is written N (I), normal moves then inverse moves in brackets, and its axis is the axis of the last move on each side. Each EO stands for its case: the last move of a side can be turned either way, and any other solution of the same case on that side is the same EO. The scramble is an ordinary random-move scramble of 25 moves, picked because it has the row's EOs, and every EO of the row was checked on it by turning the stickers. Try finding them before you look at the row.
