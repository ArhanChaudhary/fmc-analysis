# EO → DR and scramble → DR: the numbers

Generated by `src/summary.py` from the files in `out/`. Scramble-level answers: 50,000 uniformly random states (`out/scrambles.txt`, `eodrtools mkscr`, seed 2026) and the 500 scrambles of judging-a-dr; EO→DR: exact over the 1,082,565 EO-solved cosets where marked, else 10,000,000 uniformly random EO-solved states with exact table lookups. Turns are face turns (HTM); canonical sequences; an EO or a DR solution is counted when it is reached for the first time at its last turn (nissy's `-a` listing). Normal = after the scramble, inverse = after the inverse scramble, NISS = both sides (length = sum). A route through an EO = the EO, then turns that keep it, to a DR on one of the two other axes. Definitions in detail: `README.md`. Witnesses [Wn]: `out/witness.json`, every solution replayed on the sticker model.

- 4-bit packing of all_dr: 1000000 random cosets, 0 differ from the file.

- Known distributions reproduced exactly: the EO-solved part of eo-fb_dr is Cale's 1,082,565 states 1, 2, 17, 134, 1065, 8190, 54694, 267576, 560568, 187204, 3114 (mean 7.8031); the any-turn version gives torchlight's 7.7969; one-axis EO mean 9439/2048 = 4.60889 (DougCube's 4.609); best NISS EO over all axes 3.45481 on 1,000,000 random states (Joba's exact 3.455); all_dr.hist 1, 4, 50, 592, …, 476 (fixed-axis row below).
- Sticker-model replays: every solution in the 50,000-, 500- and superflip tables (NISS DR, an optimal DR with its EO split, the best routes through EOs of ≤4, 5, 6) and every witness.
- Sample against exact: fixed-axis DR length on the sample, mean 9.5266 (3 x 50,000 axes), against the exact 9.5245. best of 3 axes, normal: chi2 9.85 on 6 df against is-dr-xs-worth-it 6.4 (2,000,000 states); best of 6 (normal or inverse): chi2 9.47 on 5 df against is-dr-xs-worth-it 6.4 (2,000,000 states); one axis: chi2 5.94 on 6 df against the exact table.
- **nissy 2.0.8's `scramble` is not uniform**: its source (src/commands.c, lines 505-508) draws `ep = rand() % FACTORIAL12`, and rand() has 2^31 values, about 4.48 times 12!, so the lowest 48.3% of edge-permutation indices are 25% more likely (a modulo bias). The drawn state is the inverse of the printed scramble, and that is where DRs come out shorter: inverse best of 3 8.8733 against 9.0466 uniform, normal side 9.0172 against 9.051. The number of twisted corners per axis is also off: chi2 33.2, 34.9, 17.8 on 7 df, p = 2e-05, 1e-05, 0.01 (cause not identified). The study therefore draws its own uniform states (`eodrtools mkscr`, written as DR path + exact DR finish, inverted; chi2 at most 9.8 on its 9 tests). `out/checks_scrambles.tsv`; the nissy-scramble results are kept as `out/nissy50k_*`.
