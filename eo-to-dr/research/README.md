# EO → DR and scramble → DR: the computations

The numbers behind the fourth case study (questions A1–A3, B4–B5, C6–C7 and the claims check D of `SPEC.md`).
`out/SUMMARY.md` states every answer; this file says how each number is made. Everything is regenerated by
`run_all.sh` (about 50 minutes on 4 cores, plus 10 minutes the first time for the nissy scrambles of the uniformity check; peak memory about 3.5 GB, in `mkscr` (two tables in RAM) and in nissy's optimal solver; every heavy step capped with `systemd-run … MemoryMax=5G`). Each step was run and checked separately; `run_all.sh` itself has not been run end to end in one go.

## Model and reused tables (credit)

- Cube model: is-dr-xs-worth-it's `xstools/src/cubie.rs`, compiled into `eodrtools` unchanged by `#[path]`; its
  move and rotation data come from that study's sticker model (`research/src/cube.py`) through
  `out/tables/cubie.txt` and `moves.bin`.
- Coset tables (2,217,093,120 cosets of the DR group, (co, eo, sl) as in that study's `coords.py`):
  `out/bfs/all_dr.u8` (fewest turns of any kind to DR on U/D) and `out/bfs/eo-fb_dr.u8` (fewest turns without F/B
  quarter turns, i.e. keeping F/B EO). Other DR axes and EO axes are read through a rotation of the cube
  (`Model::conj`): DR on axis X = DR on U/D of the cube turned so X is U/D; "EO on Y then DR on X" uses a rotation
  that carries X to U/D and Y to F/B. Every one of these mappings is checked against nissy (below).
- `all_dr.u8` is held at 4 bits per coset in RAM (1.1 GB, attempt.rs's packing). Questions A2, A3 and C only ever
  look up EO-solved states, so they use the eo = 0 part of each table (1,082,565 bytes each,
  `eodrtools extract`).
- Scrambles: 50,000 uniformly random states (`eodrtools mkscr 50000 2026`: splitmix64 as in is-dr-xs-worth-it,
  each written as the inverse of a shortest path to DR plus an optimal DR finish from judging-a-dr's exact table,
  about 23 turns; `out/scrambles.txt`), and the 500 scrambles of judging-a-dr (`out/hand/attempt_scrambles.txt`).
  nissy 2.0.8's `scramble` was used first and dropped. Its source (`src/commands.c`, lines 505-508) draws
  `eo = rand() % POW2TO11; ep = rand() % FACTORIAL12; co = rand() % POW3TO7; cp = rand() % FACTORIAL8;`: with glibc's
  rand() in [0, 2^31), `rand() % FACTORIAL12` takes the lowest 48.3% of edge-permutation indices 5 times out of 2^31
  and the others 4 times, so those are 25% more likely (a modulo bias; the other three draws are close to uniform). The
  drawn state is the inverse of the printed scramble (the scramble is a solution of it), and the effect shows there:
  on 50,000 nissy scrambles the inverse best of 3 averages 8.87 against 9.05 on uniform states, the normal side 9.02
  against 9.05. The twisted-corner counts are also off (chi2 33.2, 34.9, 17.8 on 7 df, p = 2e-5, 1e-5, 0.013;
  `src/check_scrambles.py`, `out/checks_scrambles.tsv`); the cause of that is not identified.
  Those scrambles (`out/nissy_scrambles.txt`) and their results (`out/nissy50k_*`, made by running `scr` and
  `analyze_scr.py … nissy50k` on them; not part of `run_all.sh`) are kept for the record only.

## Definitions as computed

- Lengths in face turns (HTM). Sequences are canonical: no two turns of one face in a row, and of two opposite
  faces the U, R, F turn first (so R L is counted, L R is not).
- *Normal* = turns after the scramble, *inverse* = turns after the inverse scramble, *NISS* = turns on both sides;
  a NISS length is |normal| + |inverse|. "Best of 6" = best of the 3 DR axes on normal and on inverse.
- *A DR solution of length L* (A1 counts): a canonical sequence of L turns that reaches DR on an axis for the first
  time at its end. Matches `nissy solve drud -a -c -m L -M L` (checked).
- *An EO of length L*: a canonical sequence that solves EO on axis Y for the first time at its end (so its last
  turn is a quarter turn of a Y face). Matches `nissy solve eo* -a`; nissy *without* `-a` drops EOs that end in
  F' or B' and lists about half as many (recorded in `out/checks_counts.tsv`; quoted counts on the server are
  usually nissy's default). *A NISS EO*: turns on both sides, each side non-empty and ending in a quarter turn of
  Y; counts match `nissy solve eo* -N -a` together with the linear ones.
- *Route through an EO*: EO E on axis Y, then turns without quarter turns of Y to a DR on one of the two other
  axes. For a normal EO the DR is on the normal side, for an inverse EO on the inverse side, for a NISS EO on
  either side (the side the solver last wrote on is a choice of writing order). A3's "(EO, DR axis) pairs" count,
  for every EO of at most k turns and each of its two DR axes, whether that route is at most N turns.
- *E\** (A2): in an optimal normal DR, the EO it starts with is the prefix up to its last quarter turn of an axis
  Y other than the DR axis (after that the turns keep Y's EO, so the prefix is an EO); a half turn of the opposite
  face written just before that quarter turn is moved after it (F2 B = B F2). E\* = the shortest such prefix over
  every optimal DR and both Y. Checked: P(E\* ≤ k) equals P(best route through an EO of ≤ k = optimal) on every
  sample.
- *Case* xcye: x corners twisted for the DR axis, y misplaced edges for it (E-slice pieces outside the E slice
  plus as many others inside), as in Cale's "DR setup stats" and doc 6.
- *Last-move inversion* (C6c): the same EO with its last turn (an F or B quarter turn) the other way, i.e. the state
  times F2 (or B2: the two give identical distributions, checked).
- C's EO-solved states are uniform: for a random scramble and an EO chosen from its edge orientation alone, the
  state after the EO is uniform over the EO-solved states, so each of the 1,082,565 cosets (co, sl) is equally likely.

`eodrtools scr` per scramble: d on each axis normal and inverse (6 lookups); the NISS DR (pre-turns C on one side,
min |C| + d(C⁻¹·S), stopping once 2|C| ≥ best, since one side of any shorter solution is at most half of it); the
number of DR solutions of each length up to 12 on the normal side (depth-first with the table as exact pruning);
E\*; every EO of at most 6 turns on each axis on the normal side, on the inverse side and with NISS (inverse-side
turns C enumerated, then the normal side), each with the exact EO-keeping DR distance on both DR axes; the best
route through an EO of at most k = 0..6; the A3 pair counts; the optimal EO per axis (linear, inverse, NISS).

## Checks

- `cubie.rs` self-test (60 vectors from the sticker model); `sticker.py` (EO and DR read off stickers: EO-keeping
  turns keep goodness, quarter turns flip four edges, both reference axes agree, superflip is 12/12/12).
- 4-bit packing: 1,000,000 random cosets equal to the file's bytes.
- Every mapping against nissy, 1000 positions each: DR from scratch on R/L, U/D, F/B (`drrl`, `drud`, `drfb`,
  random-state scrambles); EO-keeping DR for all six (EO axis, DR axis) pairs (`drud-eofb` …, random positions
  with that EO); any-turn DR from EO-solved positions (eo = 0 part of all_dr, three axes); optimal EO per axis and
  NISS EO over all axes. Result: `out/checks_nissy.tsv`.
- Counts (DR solutions per length, linear and NISS EO counts, best of 6) against nissy on 30 scrambles, and the
  NISS DR against `nissy solve dr -N` on 40 scrambles where NISS (≤ 7) beats linear: `out/checks_counts.tsv`.
- Uniformity of the scrambles: `out/checks_scrambles.tsv`.
- Known distributions: eo = 0 part of eo-fb_dr is 1, 2, 17, 134, 1065, 8190, 54694, 267576, 560568, 187204, 3114
  (Cale's 1,082,565 states, mean 7.80); all_dr.hist 1, 4, 50, 592, …, 476; one-axis EO mean 4.609 (exact,
  9439/2048); best NISS EO over all axes 3.455 (sampled, within sampling error); best-of-3 and best-of-6 DR
  against is-dr-xs-worth-it's 2,000,000-scramble sample (`out/compare_prior.tsv`).
- Every solution in `out/big/scr*.tsv` and `out/witness.json` is replayed on the sticker model
  (`verify_scr.py`, `witness.py`).
