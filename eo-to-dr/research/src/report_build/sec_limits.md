## 7. What this does not cover

- **Findability.** Every number is what exists: the best over every EO of a given length, every DR axis, every DR after it. How much of that a solver finds in an hour is not measured. The questions about how many DRs people find, check or should check (Aneurin [[D1238]], Chris Choi [[T4676]]) are answered only for what is there.
- **RZPs.** EO-solved states are uniform over their cosets; the states a solver reaches after an RZP are not (torchlight's point in [[T4250]]). So the 4c4e odds here are for a 4c4e straight off an EO, and KrzychXyz's question whether those are better than ones set up after the EO ([[T4265]]) stays open. Switching the last move of the RZP rather than of the EO, which Cale measured as "slightly worse" ([[T4264]]), is not redone here.
- **More than the last EO turn.** Only the last quarter turn of the EO is switched. An EO that ends in `F B` can switch either; Cale's second sheet has that column and this study does not.
- **Full solutions.** The EO inside optimal full solutions (trig0n's estimate [[D504]], zakimoshi's question [[D1422]], LiamDaBestest's [[D6674]]) is a different quantity from the EO inside the optimal DR measured here.
- **Official scrambles.** Everything is on uniformly random states. Official scrambles are random-state too, but the counts people quote from Cale's and Tzak's sheets of official scrambles (and the official records) are not recomputed.
- **ARM and pairs.** Cases are by DRM only; the ARM, pairs and corner-case tables of Louis, Evan and Rodney Kinney are not extended (beyond the second DR axis, which they did not have).
- **Finishes.** Nothing here goes past DR.
- **The exact NISS EO counts per scramble** (Radek B's question, [[D18403]]) are sampled, not exact. For one axis linearly they are exact.
