**Switching late.** "this isn't the worst practice but it's pretty rare for switching after doing more than 2 moves to actually be useful, unless you're variationmaxing" (Evan, [[D11196]]).

With HTRs up to two moves over the DR's shortest NISS HTR (budget 2, the write-up's default), every optimal reading within the budget switches after 3 or more moves on {= pc(C11('all', 'budget2_needs_switch_after_gt2')) =} of DRs, about one in eight, and after 4 or more on {= pc(C11('all', 'budget2_needs_switch_after_gt3')) =} (sampled, {= n(25000) =} per subset × edges; another {= pc(C11('all', 'no_optimal_within_budget2')) =} have no optimal reading within the budget and aren't counted). So overall a switch after more than 2 moves is needed on one DR in eight, on 4b2 and 2c3 on about one in twelve (table below), and after more than 3 it is rare. Through the shortest HTR that any reading of any optimal finish reaches, every reading that reaches it switches after 3 or more moves on {= pc(C11('all', 'shortest_htr_needs_switch_after_gt2')) =} of DRs. That one is an upper bound on how often a late switch is needed for the optimal: on some of these DRs another reading, with a longer HTR, is linear or switches early.

Evan named the exceptions in the same thread: "ehh the only thing I do this for regularly is the b side of 4c3" and "4c4 and 0c4 ofc but those are pretty rare, and switching U2 F2 U reductions for some subsets can be useful but is also uncommon I feel" ([[D11237]], [[D11238]]).
