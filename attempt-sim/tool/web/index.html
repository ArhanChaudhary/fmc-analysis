Attempt simulator

Play a simulated hour on a scramble: EOs, RZPs, DRs, HTR routes and the finish, with a time cost for everything the solver looks at and the misses a person makes. You get the solution it wrote down, every backup it found on the way, and the whole attempt step by step. It's the program behind How to spend the hour; the costs and limits below are that write-up's (section 4 says where each one comes from).
